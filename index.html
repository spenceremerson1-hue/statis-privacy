Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Statis ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Statis mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information. The app uses your camera to scan TV scoreboards and your photo library if you choose to upload images. These images are sent to our servers solely to identify the game and return stats. Images are not stored.

Third Party Services

We use OpenAI to process scoreboard images and BallDontLie API for sports data. These services have their own privacy policies.

Camera and Photo Library

The app requests camera and photo library access solely to scan TV scoreboards. This data is not stored or shared beyond the immediate request.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@statis.live
