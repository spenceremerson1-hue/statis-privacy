Questions

Quick answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Statis works as soon as you open it.

Is Statis a sportsbook?

No. Statis does not accept wagers or hold money. It helps you follow games and track bets you placed elsewhere.

Which sports and books work?

NBA, NFL and MLB games. DraftKings bets import by link, screenshot, Photos, paste or the share sheet. Slips from other sportsbooks are read from a screenshot, best effort.

What if a bet can't be settled automatically?

We never guess. If a pick can't be graded with confidence, Statis asks you how the bet ended.
